## New Projects

I added three new/old projects:

[**Happy Plants**](/projects/happy-plants), as mentioned in other [blog posts](/blog/2024-year-in-review/#:~:text=I%20built%20a%20home%20cooked%20plant%20watering%20app) now has a project page. There's no public demo or source code available (_on purpose_) but at least now it is documented.

[**eBird Compress**](/projects/ebird-compress) is an oldie but a goodie. I use it all the time, check it out if you haven't already. 

[**Birds Make Sound**](/projects/birds-make-sound) is an even older oldie and goodie.

The other fun update is you can view recent [**RainCrow**](/projects/raincrow) useage [on a map](https://timezone.parker.town/map). Right now, it looks like someone is sailing along the coast of Austrailia and eBirding along the way! So cool.

## Bird Projects

I wanted there to be a page for birding folks to easily find the birding tools I've built. RainCrow has the good feature of advertising itself to other birders for every checklist its on but the other tools are a little harder to discover so: bird projects are now noted with a 🦆 and there is a [birding projects](/projects/birding) page.
